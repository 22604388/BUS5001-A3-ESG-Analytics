## Experiment Setup

**Tool evaluated:** Google NotebookLM (free tier)
**Date of evaluation:** June 2026
**Scenario:** ESG academic briefing preparation

**Sources uploaded:**
1. Woolworths Group Sustainability Report 2023
2. BUS5001 Assessment 3 brief (ESG Analytics context)

---

## Feature 1: Document Q&A (Chat Interface)

**Prompt used:** "What are the key ESG priorities outlined in this report?"

**Output summary:** NotebookLM identified material sustainability themes
including emissions reduction targets, packaging commitments, and supplier
code of conduct requirements. Responses were grounded in the uploaded
source documents with inline citations.

**Accuracy observation:** High relevance to source material. No hallucinated
statistics observed. All claims traceable to specific document sections.

---

## Feature 2: Notebook Guide (Auto-generated Summary)

**Action:** Clicked "Notebook guide" in left panel

**Output summary:** NotebookLM generated a structured overview of the
uploaded sources including key themes, suggested questions, and topic
clusters. The guide accurately reflected the ESG focus of the documents.

**Accuracy observation:** Summary was coherent and well-structured.
Occasional over-generalisation of specific targets into broader statements.

---

## Feature 3: Study Questions Generation

**Prompt used:** "Generate study questions based on these documents"

**Output summary:** Generated 8 questions covering ESG reporting frameworks,
supplier sustainability requirements, emissions scope definitions, and
governance accountability structures.

**Accuracy observation:** Questions were relevant and at an appropriate
postgraduate level. Some questions were surface-level and did not probe
deeper analytical thinking.

---

## Feature 4: Briefing Document Creation

**Prompt used:** "Create a briefing document on ESG reporting priorities
for a senior executive audience"

**Output summary:** Produced a structured briefing with an executive
summary, key findings, and recommended actions. Format was professional
and appropriate for the stated audience.

**Accuracy observation:** Content was well-grounded in the source material.
The tool did not draw on external knowledge beyond the uploaded documents,
which is both a strength (controllability) and a limitation (scope).

---

## Key Limitations Observed

- Cannot access real-time data or sources outside uploaded documents
- Occasional over-summarisation loses nuance from original sources
- No built-in plagiarism detection or academic integrity guardrails
- Audio overview feature uses synthetic voices which may reduce trust
  in formal academic settings

---

## Conclusion

NotebookLM shows strong potential as a document-grounded research
assistant for postgraduate students. It is most effective for synthesising
large document sets and generating structured outputs. It requires
critical evaluation of all outputs before academic use.
